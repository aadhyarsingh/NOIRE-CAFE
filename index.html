Our story

Built for the whole day.

NOIRÉ is imagined as a modern grand café: a place where a breakfast meeting, an afternoon coffee, a long dinner and a late dessert can all belong to the same table. The kitchen borrows freely from Italy, Asia and India while the coffee bar and pâtisserie keep the café at its centre.

Not fine dining. Not a coffee chain. Not only dessert. A café with enough range to become a ritual.

— NOIRÉ, Dehradun
